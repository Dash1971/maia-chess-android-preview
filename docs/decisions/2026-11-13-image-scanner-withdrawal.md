# Camera/image prototype withdrawal — 13 November 2026

The camera/image prototype was removed in Preview 2.6.0-beta.3. The available
small freely licensed diagram model was not reliable enough, while the accurate
comparison model was large and noncommercial. Manual Board Editor, FEN import,
Analysis and Continue from here remain available.

This decision records withdrawal, not completed acceptance of the old scanner.
Keep the [prototype record](../BOARD_IMAGE_IMPORT.md) and its unchecked limits
as history. Historical beta.2 and Dev alpha.2 APKs retain their original payloads.
See the [model investigation](https://github.com/Dash1971/mobile-maia-research/blob/main/studies/offline-board-recognition-2026-11-13/REPORT.md).
